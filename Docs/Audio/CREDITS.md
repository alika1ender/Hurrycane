# Hurrycane: audio sources and credits

Every sound in the game, where it came from, and under which licence. All Freesound pages were opened and the licence text read between 17 and 18 October 2026. Keep this file (and the download date) as the record for the competition disclosure log.

## What "CC0" means

CC0 1.0 is a public-domain dedication. You may copy, change, redistribute and use the sound commercially **without asking and without crediting the author**. Crediting is optional and polite, so the in-game credits below list everyone anyway. CC0 cannot be taken back by the uploader.

**CC BY 4.0** (three sounds, see "Needs credit" below) also allows commercial use and changes, but the author **must be credited**.

## Needs credit (CC BY 4.0)

The storm sound we use for the storm beds, the rain and the thunder is `large-storm-merged.mp3` by al-wa7sh. It is marked CC0, but the uploader says it is a mix of three other Freesound recordings, and those three are **CC BY 4.0**. A remix of CC BY material cannot be relicensed as CC0, so the three original authors have to be credited:

Licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Used in: `storm_loop_light`, `storm_loop_medium`, `storm_loop_heavy` and `thunder_01` to `thunder_06`. We changed them (cut, looped, filtered, level-adjusted).

Suggested credits line (put it in the game's credits screen and in the submission text):

**Simplest way to remove the obligation:** replace the storm sounds with CC0 recordings that are not mixes. In that case no credit is required, but credit is still welcome.

| File | How |

| `phone_vibrate.wav` | Synthesised with ffmpeg (a 165 Hz buzz with a 330 Hz overtone). No third-party material. |

| `tape_rip.wav` | Three pitch-shifted copies of `duct tape.ogg` (adharca, CC0). |
| `tv_news_mumble.wav`, `tv_news_mumble_alt.wav` | The news anchor mumble. **Generated with the Windows built-in speech voice (Microsoft Zira)**, then made unintelligible (low-passed, reverb, or reversed). Microsoft's licence for output of its built-in voices is not as clear-cut as CC0. Being unintelligible does not establish redistribution permission. Confirm the applicable voice-output terms before submission, or replace it with a recording with verified permission. |

All other files in `Resources/HurrycaneAudio` are edits of the CC0 sounds above (cut, looped, filtered, level-balanced).

## What you should do

1. Add the "Needs credit" line to the game's credits screen and to the submission text. Also retain the other source/license records and verify any unresolved permissions below.
2. Put this file (or a shortened copy) into the repo, for example `Docs/Audio/CREDITS.md`, and list the sources in the competition disclosure log.
3. Keep the download date and the Freesound links (done above). If a page disappears later, this file is your proof of the licence at the time.
4. Decide about the storm sound: credit the three authors (works, no new files), or swap in non-mixed CC0 storm recordings.
5. Resolve the news-mumble redistribution permission before submission, or replace it with a recording with verified permission.
